# Foundational Thinking DPF Suite

> Mathematical, physical and computational methods for entering unfamiliar problems, using their results and developing better ways of working.

- **Author:** Anatoly Levenchuk, with AI-assisted development and review
- **Date:** 15 September 2026
- **Status:** Eternal alpha. Mathematical Thinking and Mathematical Modeling are available; the wider repertoire is being developed.
- **Publication:** [FPF repository](https://github.com/ailev/FPF)

**Start with the [Suite Reference](FOUNDATIONAL-THINKING-DPF-SUITE-REFERENCE.md)** when you need to choose among these contributions or connect them. Open a DPF directly when you already know the mathematical or modeling operation you need.

## Start from a connected problem

You may know formulas or individual operations and still be unable to connect them into an answer. The Suite helps make those connections: what information a construction must retain, what another method can do with its result, and where a changed question makes earlier work worth revisiting.

| What you need to do | Follow the worked connection |
| --- | --- |
| Split or change a calculation while keeping its answer meaningful | [Change a combining rule](MATHEMATICAL-PRACTICE-DPF.md#mp-combine-results---change-a-rule-so-that-separately-obtained-results-can-be-combined): expose the lost distinction, construct a summary and operation, prove their compatibility, then use and revise them. |
| Decide a question without reconstructing every hidden detail | [Obtain a sufficient answer](MATHEMATICAL-MODELING-PRACTICE-DPF.md#mmp-sufficient-answer---answer-the-working-question-before-reconstructing-every-detail): expose what reduction loses, bound its effect, interpret the result and obtain more where it changes the answer. |
| Connect mathematics to changes in working methods | [Understand and change a construction](FOUNDATIONAL-THINKING-DPF-SUITE-REFERENCE.md#32-understand-and-change-a-construction): retain the distinction a later operation uses and connect it to Method Engineering. |

These examples use several patterns together. Their short sequences show dependencies; worked explanations give calculations and return points. Start at a later contribution when its inputs are available. Each DPF's Table of Contents also gives direct help for other questions.

## Purpose and continuity

The Suite is constituted for people and teams working with theories, models, specialists and AI: enter an unfamiliar question, develop and connect mathematical, physical and computational contributions, interpret the consequence, and use it to continue inquiry or change a way of working. Its members may also be used independently.

A DPF series qualifies for inclusion when it develops a substantial repertoire contributing to that purpose, has a usable public edition, and explains the results and conditions another contribution can use. Subject examples demonstrate that reach. A collection concerned only with a particular device or chapter of a science needs its corresponding narrower publication boundary.

An accepted architecture decision adds or removes a product series and updates this account. A later edition of an included series replaces the edition presented here when it retains the series' field purpose, identifies its changes, provides usable methods and source access, and states consequential limits. A substantially different field purpose requires a decision about the series itself. Changes to method details, pattern count, profiles or file arrangement can preserve the Suite's identity while its shared purpose continues.

If an edition becomes unavailable or cannot support a previously advised use, the Reference must expose that limitation when presenting the affected answer. Direct use of another known contribution can continue. If a series ceases to serve the shared purpose, the architecture decision settles repair, relocation or removal; earlier editions and the reasons for changed advice remain identifiable through publication history. Ending the shared purpose retires this Suite. A later collection with a different purpose needs its own constitution.

## The Reference and its editions

The Reference is separately constituted as a publication series belonging to this Suite. Its readers need a useful answer about selection, combination, shared architecture or an unavailable contribution. It returns to this membership account and to the publications that supply the answer. It is published as one Markdown file alongside the member DPFs.

A later Reference edition is admitted when its answers agree with the editions it presents, its available and planned contributions are distinguishable, and its readers can return to those sources. A different audience or use that defeats these conditions requires a decision about a different Reference series. A superseded or retired edition remains historical; its date alone cannot establish that its advice still applies.
