The September 2026 five-pattern edition has been withdrawn. Its selected techniques for physical balances, conductance networks, observation association and shared-state interaction did not provide the general mathematical-modeling methods promised by the title.

The replacement is being developed around constructing, interpreting, using and revising models across different kinds of problems, and their connection to mathematical, physical and computational thinking. There is currently no replacement MMP edition available at this address. The former bodies remain preserved in development history for reuse where their actual scope fits.

For current common methods of inquiry and interpretation, see [FPF](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md), particularly B.5 and C.29. Those methods provide part of the needed repertoire; they do not constitute a replacement MMP publication.
